  ## Medium

  **1. Editing an absence that has started checks the cover against days already past**
  - Where: `src/Rcm.Api/Domain/AbsenceRules.cs:146-152` (`CoverRule`), called from `Validate` at line 63.
  - What happens: once an absence has started, its start date cannot change, so `change.StartDate` is a past date. The "cover is away" check still runs over the whole start-to-end range.
  - Scenario: Blake is away Nov 17–23 with Alex as cover. On Nov 18 Sam records a one-day absence for Alex, which nothing prevents. From Nov 19 on, every edit to Blake's absence fails with "Alex is away on Nov 18": extending it, shortening it, or changing the note. A started absence cannot be deleted, so the only way out is to drop or swap the cover, and Alex can never be set as cover again.
  - Spec: docs/02, Covers. The rule says "away on any of those days", but the decision there says a cover only grants rights for days still ahead, so past days should not count.
  - Confidence: confirmed by tracing the code. I could not probe it, because it needs the clock to move forward a day.
