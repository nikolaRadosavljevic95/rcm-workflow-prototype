  ## Rules (`src/Rcm.Api/Domain`, pure, no EF)
  - `Result.cs`: `Result` and `Result<T>` with `ErrorKind` Invalid, Forbidden, NotFound, Conflict and a message.
  - `WorkflowAction.cs`: Start, Block, Unblock, Submit, Approve, Return.
  - `ActorContext`: user id, role, ids of the people the actor covers today (empty in step 2).
  - `ItemSnapshot`: status, owner id, submitter id, month open, due date.
  - `ReviewerRules`: the reviewer is the owner's `ReportsToId`. If the owner reports to no one, any manager other than the owner. Exposes `IsReviewer(actor, item)`, where a reviewer's cover counts.
  - `WorkflowRules`:
    - `AllowedActions(actor, item)` gives the actions to show.
    - `Decide(actor, item, action, text)` returns `Result<Decision>`, where the decision holds the new status and the field changes.
    - Checks run in this order: closed month gives 400. An action not valid from the current status gives 400. An actor without the right gives 403. Missing text for Block, Submit or Return gives 400.
    - Owner actions are for the owner or the owner's cover. Approve and Return are for the reviewer, the reviewer's cover or any manager, but never the submitter.

  ## Rules (`src/Rcm.Api/Domain`, pure, no EF)
  - `Result.cs`: `Result` and `Result<T>` with `ErrorKind` Invalid, Forbidden, NotFound, Conflict and a message.
  - `WorkflowAction.cs`: Start, Block, Unblock, Submit, Approve, Return.
  - `ActorContext`: user id, role, ids of the people the actor covers today (empty in step 2).
  - `ItemSnapshot`: status, owner id, submitter id, month open, due date.
  - `ReviewerRules`: the reviewer is the owner's `ReportsToId`. If the owner reports to no one, any manager other than the owner. Exposes `IsReviewer(actor, item)`, where a reviewer's cover counts.
  - `WorkflowRules`:
    - `AllowedActions(actor, item)` gives the actions to show.
    - `Decide(actor, item, action, text)` returns `Result<Decision>`, where the decision holds the new status and the field changes.
    - Checks run in this order: closed month gives 400. An action not valid from the current status gives 400. An actor without the right gives 403. Missing text for Block, Submit or Return gives 400.
    - Owner actions are for the owner or the owner's cover. Approve and Return are for the reviewer, the reviewer's cover or any manager, but never the submitter.
    - Field effects: Block sets `BlockedReason`, Unblock clears it, Submit sets `Summary`, `SubmittedById` and `SubmittedAt`, Approve sets `ApprovedById` and `ApprovedAt`, Return leaves the summary and puts the comment on the event.
  - `VisibilityRules`:
    - `CanSee(actor, owner)`: a manager sees all. A supervisor sees their own items, their direct reports' items, and the items of people they cover and those people's reports. A biller sees their own items and those of people they cover.
    - `InMyWork`: the owner is me or someone I cover.
    - `AwaitingMyReview`: the item is Submitted, I am the reviewer or cover the reviewer, and I am not the submitter.
  - `ItemFlags`: Overdue when today is after the due date and the status is not Approved or Incomplete. Missed last month when the previous item ended Incomplete. Today comes from `BusinessCalendar`.

  ## API (`src/Rcm.Api/Features`)
  - `Http/ResultMapping.cs`: maps `Result` to ProblemDetails (400, 403, 404, 409). The endpoints use it and hold no logic.
  - `Cycles/`: `GET /api/cycles`, newest first, to fill the month picker. Opening and closing stay in step 4.
  - `WorkItems/`:
    - `WorkItemContext` loads one month's items with facility, owner, previous item and users, then builds snapshots and rows in memory.
    - `GET /api/work-items?cycleId=&view=mine` returns `{ cycle, needsAction, waitingOrDone }`. Needs action means Returned, Blocked, Not started or In progress. Each group is sorted by AR over 90, highest first, with empty balances last.
    - `GET /api/work-items?cycleId=&view=overview` is for supervisors and managers only; billers get 403. It returns `{ cycle, waitingForMyReview, needsAttention, people, items }`. Managers also get `progress` (count per status) and `teams` (per supervisor: items, approved, submitted, open, overdue). Needs attention means Blocked, Returned, Overdue or Missed last month.
    - Each row carries id, facility, owner, reviewer (name, or "Any manager"), team, status, flags, balances in cents, `allowedActions`, `awaitingMyReview` and `version`.
    - `cycleId` defaults to the open month, else the latest.
    - `GET /api/work-items/{id}` returns the row fields plus summary, blocked reason, events (actor, on behalf of, type, from and to, text, time) and last month's status and summary. An item the user cannot see gives 404.
    - `POST /api/work-items/{id}/transitions` takes `{ action, text, version }`. Order of checks: 404 when the item is not visible, 409 when the version is stale, then the rule result. The handler applies the decision, bumps `Version`, adds a StatusChanged event and saves both together. A concurrency exception on save also gives 409.
    - `POST /api/work-items/{id}/comments` takes `{ text }`. It needs a visible item, an open month and non-empty text, adds a Commented event and leaves `Version` alone.
  - Text is limited to the column lengths (400 when longer). Comment and summary text is never logged.
